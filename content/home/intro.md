👋 Hi, I’m Josh. I live in Alabama with my wife and our two kids, and I build software for a living.

I’ve been messing with computers for about as long as I can remember. I was swapping out the Windows shell on the family Gateway when I was ten and bought _C++ for Dummies_ on a band trip in high school. Somehow it still took me until my thirties to figure out that I should probably do this for a living.

I took the scenic route getting there. I came to the University of Alabama to study music education and play trombone in the Million Dollar Band, eventually graduated in civil engineering, and spent a few years doing other things before finding my way into IT and then software. Once I got the chance to build software professionally, I pretty much took the reins and never let go.

Open source is where a lot of that curiosity ends up. Partly because I learned this stuff by drafting off people who put their own work out there, and it feels wrong to only take. Mostly, though, it’s where I go to play. I’ll get interested in something, disappear down a rabbit hole, and sometimes come back with a Django package or a language server or some other thing that probably didn’t need to exist until I decided it did.

This is where I put the things I make, the things I’m learning, and whatever else I feel like writing about.
